Do Kalshi prices match what happened?

An actual-versus-expected study of 17,653 settled NFL and NBA player-prop contracts from 88 games, 5 October to 9 November 2026. By Harrison Hong.

  1. No measurable gap between prices and outcomes. Every price band paid out within -2.0 to +0.9 points of what its price implied, and no band's 95% range excludes zero. The data cannot rule out a gap of up to 4.5 points in a band.
  2. The loss was mostly the cost of trading. Buying YES on every contract at the quoted ask would have returned -9.9% (95% range -14.2% to -5.9%). The half-spread and the fee, both known before buying, were 75% of that loss. This is a hypothetical return, not a trading record.
  3. It held when any one game was dropped. Leaving out each game in turn keeps the return between -11.0% to -9.2%.

What this does not show

  • The sample is five weeks of one season. Only the bands from 50c up reach full credibility.
  • The games are the ones my app scanned. They are not a random sample of Kalshi.
  • Returns are at prices on screen with an assumed order size. They are not trades, and real fills could be worse.
  • Single-league results are exploratory. With two leagues and six bands, some ranges exclude zero by chance.
  • This is a study of prices. It contains no picks and is not betting advice.

Method, robustness checks, code and tests are on GitHub. There is also a one-page summary (PDF).
